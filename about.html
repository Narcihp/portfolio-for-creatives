  • About Narci

    I hold a Bachelor's degree in Design in Visual Communication with interdisciplinary contentration.

    I go by Narci since moving to beautiful Vancouver, BC, where I also completed the last two years of high school before earning my Bachelor’s Degree.

    I like to bring storytelling and emotion into my designs when it feels right, creating work that connects on both a visual and emotional level. My main interests are in product packaging and illustration, where I can explore narrative, texture, and meaning through designs I create.

    When I’m not designing, you’ll likely find me taking photos on the street, exploring matcha cafés with mt friends, sketching ideas for my next project, or most likely taking a nap with my cat — Sora (his name means “sky” in Japanese).
