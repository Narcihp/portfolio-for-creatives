Nowruz Spendor

This was one of the first projects that helped me discover my illustration style. The poster was created for an Iranian community event celebrating the New Year.

The sketches that made it to the finish line!

From all the early explorations, these two sketches captured the mood and direction I wanted. I refined them and began colouring to develop the final Nowruz Splendor poster.
