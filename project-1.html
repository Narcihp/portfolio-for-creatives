Bloma

Bloma is a natural paint brand that uses earth-based materials to create its pigments.

Brand overview

Bloma is designed with a focus on clarity, calm, and intention. The brand treats paint as both a functional product and a design object, where color, form, and usability work together. Every detail—from packaging structure to typography and material choices—is considered to create a simple, modern experience that feels thoughtful, accessible, and visually refined.

How were the colours chosen and shades created?

The colours and shades were developed by testing natural elements alongside hands-on experimentation at home and on paper. Materials, textures, and tones inspired by nature were explored and refined in different lighting and environments, allowing the palette to form organically and feel grounded, calm, and authentic.
