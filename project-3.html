Calm Commute

A sensory-friendly app designed for individuals with sensory challenges, helping them commute more comfortably and with less stress.

How? It provides tools like planned trips, distraction games, checklists, and short meditations to make commuting easier and more manageable.

Problem

Many people, who commute to work and school, silently struggle with overstimulation caused by crowded and noisy environments. This constant exposure to sensory overload can lead to feelings of stress, anxiety, and exhaustion, making their daily commute physically and mentally challenging.

Outcome

A platform app designed to empower sensory-sensitive individuals by providing tools and features that make their commute calmer and less stimulating.

Home Page

The home page provides a calming, easy-to-navigate interface with quick access to commute tracking, activities, and personalized settings.
