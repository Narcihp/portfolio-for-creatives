Ahlan

Ahlan is a coffee packaging project inspired by my knowledge and experience with Middle Eastern cultures. My goal was to highlight the beauty, richness, and storytelling of the region through design.

Brand overview

Ahlan is a coffee packaging project inspired by Middle Eastern culture. The brand highlights the region’s beauty through storytelling, patterns, and warm visual elements. Each blend is designed to reflect the spirit of its cultural roots, creating a meaningful connection between the coffee and the traditions behind it.

Ahlan celebrates hospitality, heritage, and the richness of Middle Eastern design.

Brand identity

Ahlan’s brand identity reflects Middle Eastern warmth and culture through traditional patterns, warm colours, and simple, modern typography. It’s welcoming, expressive, and rooted in storytelling.

Logo & Branding

The logo uses thick and thin flowy lines inspired by Middle Eastern calligraphy. The branding carries the same rhythm and softness, creating an elegant and culturally rooted visual identity.

Our story

Ahlan began with a simple idea: coffee can carry culture. Inspired by the places, people, and traditions of the Middle East, the project turns regional stories into visual design. Each package is a small tribute to the colours, patterns, and warmth of the region.

What does “Ahlan” mean?

An Arabic word that means "welcome" or "hello," often used as a warm and friendly greeting. It can also imply hospitality, as it conveys a sense of making someone feel at home.
